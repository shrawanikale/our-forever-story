

# 💍 "Our Forever Story" — Cinematic Love Website

A premium, emotional storytelling website that unfolds your love journey through 7 poetic chapters with elegant photo galleries, smooth animations, and a surprise proposal finale.

---

## 🎨 Design Foundation
- **Color palette**: Soft pastels — blush pink, lavender, cream, transitioning to deep midnight blue for the finale
- **Typography**: Elegant serif font (Playfair Display) for headings, clean sans-serif (Inter) for body text
- **Animations**: Fade-ins, parallax scrolling, subtle floating sparkle particles
- **Layout**: Full-screen smooth-scroll storytelling, fully mobile responsive

---

## 🎵 Music Player
- Elegant "Play Our Song" floating button on the hero screen (no autoplay)
- Player UI ready for you to add your audio file later
- Soft toggle to pause/play throughout the experience

---

## 🌸 Chapter 1 — The Bloom (Hero)
- Full-screen romantic hero image with cinematic zoom-in animation
- Poetic overlay text about quiet beginnings
- "Begin Our Story" button that smooth-scrolls into the journey

## 📸 Chapter 2 — The Moments That Built Us (Memory Grid)
- Responsive photo grid (3 columns desktop, 1 mobile) with ~6 images
- Rounded corners, soft shadows, hover zoom
- Lightbox modal for full-size viewing with captions
- Fade-in animations as you scroll

## 🖼 Chapter 3 — The Sweet & Silly Us (Polaroid Strip)
- Horizontal scrolling polaroid-style photos (~4 images)
- Slightly tilted with handwritten-style captions
- Warm, nostalgic aesthetic

## 🎞 Chapter 4 — Falling Again & Again (Cinematic Slider)
- Slow auto-playing image slider (~4 images, 5-second intervals)
- Fade transitions with blurred background
- Emotional overlay poetry text

## 🤍 Chapter 5 — The Comfort (Featured Photos)
- Minimal layout with 3 large featured images
- Deep emotional captions beneath each
- Subtle parallax scroll effect

## 🤝 Chapter 6 — The Promise
- Soft lavender/cream background
- Animated text reveal about commitment
- A few meaningful memory photos below (~2 images)
- Subtle glow behind text

## 🌙 Chapter 7 — The Realization
- Background transitions to deep midnight blue
- Slowly appearing text lines about finding your person
- Dramatic pause animations between lines

## 💍 The Forever Moment (Finale)
- Starry night sky background
- Text appears line by line with pauses
- Animated ring reveal
- Glowing "Will you build forever with me?" button
- **YES**: Golden glow, elegant minimal confetti, final full-screen couple image, closing message
- **NO**: Button playfully dodges, sweet persistent message

---

## ✨ Photo Handling
- ~18 elegant romantic placeholder images across all sections
- All images easily replaceable (just swap the file/URL)
- Lazy loading for smooth performance
- Clean spacing, no visual clutter
